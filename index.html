WHAT’S INSIDE

A

PALM / REVERB

An eight-line hall with slow internal shimmer: the wide, chorus-y digital reverb of late-80s records. Goes from a tight plate to a twenty-second wash.

Size · Decay · Damping · Shimmer · Pre-delay · Width · Mix · Duck · Gate

B

AGE / VHS

Puts the sound on an old tape: warped pitch drift, transport flutter, soft saturation, hiss and a narrowing frequency range. Age everything, or only the reverb tail.

Wow · Flutter · Warmth · Hiss · Fade · Output

C

DRIFT / AUTO PAN

Moves the sound between the speakers, free-running or locked to your song’s tempo. Pan the whole sound, or keep your dry signal centred while only the reverb drifts around it.

Rate · Depth · Sync · Sine / Triangle / Square / Random

FAQ

WHICH DAWS WORK?

Any Windows DAW that loads VST3 plug-ins, including Ableton Live, FL Studio, Reaper, Cubase, Studio One and Bitwig.

IS THERE A MAC VERSION?

It’s on the way. Buyers will get it free when it’s released.

HOW DO I INSTALL IT?

Copy the Palm Life.vst3 folder into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 and rescan plug-ins in your DAW. If Windows shows a warning, choose More info, then Run anyway. Then click REGISTER and paste your key.

ARE UPDATES FREE?

Yes, every 1.x update. Palm Life shows an UPDATE button when one is out, and you download it from your customer portal.

NEW COMPUTER?

In Palm Life click LICENCE, then DEACTIVATE, and register on the new one. You can also free up computers from your customer portal.
