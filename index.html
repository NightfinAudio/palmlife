WHAT’S INSIDE

Four sections plus a lush chorus and phaser. Use them all together, or switch any section off and it leaves your sound untouched.

A

PALM / REVERB

The wide, shimmering digital reverb of late-80s records. Goes from a tight plate to a twenty-second wash, with a gate for the classic 80s drum sound.

Size · Decay · Damping · Shimmer · Pre-delay · Width · Mix · Duck · Gate

B

AGE / VHS

Puts the sound on an old tape: wobbling pitch, flutter, soft saturation, hiss, dropouts and a narrowing tone. The hiss comes in three kinds: tape, VHS, or the static, buzz and whine of an old TV set. Age everything, or only the reverb tail.

Wow · Flutter · Warmth · Hiss · Fade · Output · Hiss: Tape / VHS / TV

C

DRIFT / AUTO PAN

Moves the sound between the speakers, free or locked to your song’s tempo. Pan everything, or keep the dry sound centred while only the reverb drifts.

Rate · Depth · Sync · Sine / Triangle / Square / Random

D

MASTER

The big Nostalgia knob pushes everything towards a lush, faraway memory. Plus the mixing tools: tail EQ, tempo-synced pre-delay and sidechain ducking.

Nostalgia · Low Cut · High Cut · Pre-delay Sync · Duck Key

+

CHORUS / PHASER

One button opens the lush section: a chorus with three characters and a slow, liquid phaser, on everything or only the reverb. The faceplate glows in time with it.

Chorus · Dimension · Ensemble · Rate · Depth · Feedback · Mix

THE SOUND

  • 43 factory presetsSorted by instrument, plus rooms like a tiled bathroom, outside the club and under the bleachers on prom night.
  • NostalgiaOne knob for chorus, shimmer and bloom at once. Turn it up and any sound turns into a memory.
  • RewindHold the button and the tape brakes, then spins backwards with that VHS squeal. Let go and it drops straight back in. Automate it to hit on the beat.
  • Gated reverb you can seeA huge room that slams shut after every hit. The Gate reads in milliseconds, a GATE light flashes and the shape display marks the cut.

MADE FOR MIXING

  • Tail EQ and tempo syncLow and high cut on the reverb, and pre-delay locked to your song’s tempo.
  • DuckingBig reverbs that step aside while the vocal sings, from the input or a sidechain.
  • A/B compare and undoTwo settings side by side, Copy A>B, and full undo and redo.
  • Switch sections offOnly want the auto-pan? Turn A and B off and they leave your sound untouched.

EASY TO USE

  • Simple or fullSIMPLE shows the essentials, big. FULL shows every control. One click between them.
  • Preset browserCategories, search and favourites, with a short note on every sound.
  • Built-in helpPoint at any control and the help bar says what it does. Double-click to reset a knob, hold Shift for fine control, or click a value to type one.
  • A faceplate that movesSpinning cassette reels, a live reverb monitor, a decay-shape display and knobs that glow with your sound.
  • Four looks, any sizeStudio, Midnight, Sunset and Dusk. Scale the window from 75% to 200%.
  • One-click updatesWhen a new version is out, click UPDATE NOW and Palm Life installs it for you.

FAQ

WHICH DAWS WORK?

Any DAW that loads VST3 or, on a Mac, Audio Units: Logic Pro, GarageBand, Ableton Live, FL Studio, Reaper, Cubase, Studio One and Bitwig. There’s also a standalone app if you just want to play through it.

HOW DO I INSTALL IT?

Unzip the download and double-click Palm Life Setup (Windows) or Palm Life Installer (Mac). It puts the plug-in in the right folders for you. If Windows shows a warning, choose More info, then Run anyway. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW, then click REGISTER and paste your key.

ARE UPDATES FREE?

Yes, every 1.x update. Palm Life shows an UPDATE button when one is out: click UPDATE NOW, allow the Windows prompt and restart your DAW. Your licence and presets carry over.

IS THERE A MAC VERSION?

Yes. The same licence works on Windows and Mac. Double-click Palm Life Installer. If your Mac says it can’t verify the developer, open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.

NEW COMPUTER?

In Palm Life click LICENCE, then DEACTIVATE, and register on the new one. You can also free up computers from your customer portal.

REFUNDS?

Full refund within 14 days if Palm Life doesn’t work on your system. Email palmlifesupport@gmail.com. Refunded licence keys are deactivated.
